# Playtest Notes

## First Version Checklist

- [ ] The full loop from Waiting Room to floor and back plays without crashes.
- [ ] Pension and upgrades survive quitting and reopening the game.
- [ ] A new player usually dies before beating the floor boss.
- [ ] A few rounds of upgrades make a win realistic.
- [ ] A normal run takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
- [ ] The cane combat feels good.

## Session Notes

- Round 1: item naming and moving between rooms feel good. Side room return door moved behind the player. Charge moved to holding attack, and right click now throws the cane. More content is planned.
- Round 2: cane throw and tap-and-hold charge feel better. Fixed a bug where the thrown cane stayed spinning instead of dropping.
- Round 3: added an optometrist selling glasses that show enemy health, and a large boss health bar.
- Round 4: added hit flash, hit stop, screen shake, death bursts, clearer attack warnings, player hurt feedback, flying coins and placeholder sounds.

- Round 8: Grandpa's collision box moved to his feet. Added Permission Slips, Overheated Thermoses, breakable blocks, loot pockets, locked chests and a locked treasure room. Next rounds: a shop paid in Pension, secret rooms, then special items with mana. Still to judge: drop rates, Thermos strength, and whether rewards make runs too easy.
